Sp. z o.o. Formation

Full registration of a Polish limited liability company (Spółka z ograniczoną odpowiedzialnością). We prepare articles of association, register with KRS and obtain NIP/REGON numbers.

  • Articles of association (umowa spółki)
  • KRS registration (National Court Register)
  • NIP and REGON numbers
  • Opening a company bank account
  • VAT registration (if applicable)
👤

JDG / Sole Trader Registration

Quick registration of a sole trader business (Jednoosobowa Działalność Gospodarcza) via CEIDG. Ideal for freelancers, consultants and small businesses.

  • CEIDG online registration
  • PKD code selection
  • Tax form selection (ryczałt, podatek liniowy)
  • ZUS social insurance setup
📋

KRS Changes & Updates

Administrative support for changes to existing companies registered in KRS — management changes, address updates, share capital modifications and more.

  • Management board changes
  • Registered address updates
  • Share capital increases
  • Shareholder changes
  • Company name changes

Our Story

Who We Are

Status Pro Legalis was founded in Warsaw with a clear mission: to help businesses navigate the Polish market and to build meaningful international B2B partnerships.

Starting with administrative and registration services for foreign nationals and companies entering Poland, we have expanded our activities to include international trade representation, industrial product supply and strategic business development across European markets.

Today we work with manufacturers, distributors, investors and project companies from across Europe, Ukraine, Turkey and beyond — providing the local expertise, international network and multilingual communication that our clients need to succeed.

Focus Areas

Our Innovation Agenda

♻️

Construction Waste Recycling

Technologies and processes for recycling demolition and construction waste into new building materials. This rapidly growing sector aligns with EU Green Deal requirements and offers significant commercial opportunities.

🔄

Circular Economy

Business models and technologies that eliminate waste through the continual use of resources. We facilitate partnerships between companies adopting circular practices and those developing enabling technologies.

🧱

Sustainable Building Materials

Low-carbon and recycled content building materials for the construction industry. We identify manufacturers of innovative materials and connect them with distributors and construction companies in Europe.

🌱

Green Technology Transfer

Facilitating the transfer of environmental technologies between markets — connecting innovators in Ukraine, Turkey and other markets with European buyers and investment.

🤝

Joint Venture Development

Supporting the creation of joint ventures and strategic alliances between European and non-European companies in the sustainability and construction sectors.

🏗️

Infrastructure & Reconstruction

Post-war reconstruction in Ukraine creates enormous demand for sustainable construction materials and technologies. We help companies access this growing market responsibly.

Electric Hoists

Wire rope and chain electric hoists for overhead crane applications. Standard and explosion-proof versions available.

  • Capacity range: 0.5t – 32t
  • Wire rope and chain variants
  • Standard and EX (ATEX) versions
  • FEM/ISO duty class options
  • Hook, grab and magnet configurations
🔧

Monorail Lifting Systems

Complete monorail beam trolley and hoist systems for assembly lines, maintenance areas and workshop lifting applications.

  • Electric and manual trolleys
  • I-beam and enclosed track types
  • Push, geared and motorized travel
  • Curved track configurations
🚂

End Carriages (Bogies)

End carriage assemblies for overhead bridge cranes. Standard and custom-gauge configurations for EOT and HOT cranes.

  • Single and double girder types
  • Standard span gauges
  • Motorized and non-motorized
  • Rail clamping and buffer sets
